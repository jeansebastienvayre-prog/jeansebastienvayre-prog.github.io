Supervision & Assessment

Postdoctoral, doctoral, Master’s and undergraduate supervision

All programme names, supervision roles and degree levels are presented in English. Thesis and report titles are retained in French, their original language. French degree distinctions are translated descriptively and the original French mention is retained in parentheses.
